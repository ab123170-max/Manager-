Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

ScanMe AI is a free AI-powered storekeeping and inventory tool for grocery stores, pharmacies, medical stores, hotels, restaurants and other businesses. This policy explains how information is collected, used and protected.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name and email address when you create an account.
  • Profile information and images that you choose to upload.
  • Inventory and product information that you enter or scan, such as product name, price, manufacturing date, expiry date, best-before period, barcode and QR information.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as browser/device information, IP address and basic usage information.
  • Information you voluntarily send through our contact channels.

How we use information

We use information to provide authentication, scanning, inventory management, expiry alerts, account features, security, troubleshooting, service improvement and support.

AI and scanned content

Images and product information submitted for scanning may be processed by the AI/scanning services used by ScanMe AI to extract product information. Users should not upload confidential information unless they are authorized to do so.

Supabase and service providers

ScanMe AI uses third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Supabase for authentication, database and storage services and Google services for advertising and related functionality. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to provide the service and subject to their applicable policies.

Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

ScanMe AI may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, analytics and advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this site and other sites. Where required by law, users are provided with consent choices for personalized advertising and related technologies. Users can manage advertising preferences through Google's available controls.

Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Users may request account/data deletion through the contact method below. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

ScanMe AI is intended for business and general users and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the ScanMe AI operator through the contact details provided on the Contact page.
