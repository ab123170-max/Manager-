About ScanMe AI

ScanMe AI is a free AI-assisted storekeeping and inventory tool designed for grocery stores, pharmacies and medical stores, hotels, restaurants and other businesses that manage products and stock.

What it provides

  • AI-assisted product scanning
  • Barcode and QR scanning
  • Inventory and stock management
  • Manufacturing and expiry-date tracking
  • Expiry and low-stock monitoring
  • Reports and stock records
